## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored for this product, not category-interchangeable.** Native GH₵ across three money surfaces; a fee-transparency line that mirrors the backend fee snapshot; a Backers wall with donor medallions, a "Biggest backer" spotlight, and an anonymous-donor reconciliation; a fund modal that anchors the donation inside the campaign's goal (closing-the-gap psychology); community copy throughout. Honors DESIGN.md (flat cards, amber primary, green reserved for success, sentence case, verb-first CTAs, stable modal height).

## Overall Impression

The browse → detail → fund flow is warm, trustworthy, and unusually well-crafted at its emotional peak (the receipt). The biggest opportunities are concentrated on the creator's "Manage" surface: withdrawing real money with no confirmation, delete enabled when it's guaranteed to fail, and an amber money-CTA that's styled as a secondary outline button.

## What's Working

1. **The Backers section is the best trust machinery on the platform** — deterministic warm medallions, a "Biggest backer" hero, a 3-stat summary, and the honest "some gave anonymously" reconciliation line.
2. **The fund modal handles its high-stakes centerpiece responsibly** — stable container height, shared amount source-of-truth, fee breakdown, anonymous toggle, on-error return to amount (never a stuck spinner), and a receipt that hands over a copyable reference.
3. **The detail page's progress semantics are honest** — overfunding keeps the % climbing while the bar caps, and the "completed" status is display-only without mutating backend state.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Withdraw has no confirmation, delete does — asymmetric stakes.**
- **Why:** withdrawing moves real money and is effectively irreversible from the user's view, yet is the *least* guarded action; the most consequential, least reversible act has the least friction.
- **Fix:** wrap withdraw in the same AlertDialog pattern as delete, restating the amount and available balance ("Move GH₵2,500 out of your campaign?").
- **Command:** /impeccable harden

**[P1] Delete is enabled with funds available, guaranteeing a server-side rejection.**
- **Why:** the dialog warns "withdraw it before deleting" but the confirm stays enabled; the backend forbids delete until all funds are withdrawn, so the user hits a failure they weren't warned about.
- **Fix:** disable the delete confirm when `available > 0` and make the withdraw-first instruction the CTA, or hide delete behind a fully-withdrawn state.
- **Command:** /impeccable harden

**[P2] Withdraw styled as an outline button undermines its stakes.**
- **Why:** DESIGN.md reserves amber primary for the single money CTA; a money-moving action as a secondary ghost reads low-importance right where the creator should pause.
- **Fix:** give Withdraw the amber primary treatment (it's the view's money CTA when Fund isn't present), and knock Edit to a quieter style so one action stands out.
- **Command:** /impeccable colorize + /impeccable layout

**[P2] Fund preset pills have no `aria-pressed`, unlike the category pills.**
- **Why:** selection state is visual-only (2px amber border); a screen-reader user can't tell which preset is selected — the most important decision in the product.
- **Fix:** add `aria-pressed={selected}` to each preset button, mirroring the browse row.
- **Command:** /impeccable harden

**[P2] Backers wall is unbounded — every donation maps with no limit.**
- **Why:** a very successful campaign produces a multi-thousand-row list that pushes content down and hurts performance.
- **Fix:** cap the rendered wall (e.g. show the top N then "and X more"), or paginate.
- **Command:** /impeccable optimize

**[P3] `FEE_PERCENT = 5` duplicates backend business logic in the client.**
- **Why:** if the platform fee changes server-side, the modal silently misquotes costs at the moment of the donation decision.
- **Fix:** expose a fee/settings read endpoint or fetch it with the campaign; at minimum centralize the constant with a TODO tied to the endpoint.
- **Command:** /impeccable harden

**[P3] Decorative amber blobs repeat across four surfaces; `font-semibold` exceeds the type spec.**
- **Why:** cheapens the "one deliberate warm accent" into a repeated crutch, and drifts from DESIGN.md's 400/500 type contract.
- **Fix:** keep the motif on the fund modal and one structural accent; reduce elsewhere; swap semibold headings/amounts to medium-500.
- **Command:** /impeccable distill + /impeccable typeset

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-time donor):** the fee line raises an unanswered question ("what does the fee pay for?"); no reassurance copy near confirm ("you'll get a reference number") — the confidence lives only *after* the donation.

**Casey (mobile donor):** the 10-pill category row hides beyond the horizontal scroll fold with no fade/arrow affordance; backers 3-col stats get tight under ~360px.

**Sam (accessibility):** fund presets lack `aria-pressed` → selection unperceivable; `text-[13px]` fee line is small for low vision.

**Alex (creator power-user):** Manage card = three flat full-width buttons with no hierarchy; withdraw (money, irreversible) least guarded; delete-with-funds is a guaranteed mid-cleanup server error.

## Minor Observations

- Edit page loading is bare "Loading…" text while detail/browse use skeletons — inconsistent loading language.
- "fundraiser" vs "campaign" naming drifts across browse, forms, buttons, empty states, routes.
- Search is client-side over an in-memory list; no sort control and no URL state — filters reset on refresh, no shareable filtered view.
- Create/edit forms are near-duplicates (only preview-seeding and goal-lock differ).

## Questions to Consider

1. The withdraw-first delete warning is text, not enforcement — is that deliberate politeness or an unguarded footgun?
2. The receipt is the strongest emotional beat, yet the reassurance lives *after* the money decision — could one calm sentence move confidence to the amount step?
3. Why call the same object a "fundraiser" on browse and a "campaign" everywhere you create/manage it?
